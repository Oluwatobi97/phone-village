# Product Specification
Phone Village is a Nigerian marketplace for buyers, individual sellers, and phone dealers to discover and list phones, especially used devices.

## User types
- Buyer
- Individual seller
- Dealer (storefront only if approved)
- Moderator/admin with restricted tools

## MVP candidates
- Public listings and detail pages
- Search, filter, and sort
- Seller profiles and listing management after auth approval
- Favourites and buyer enquiries
- Dealer storefronts only if prioritised
- Reporting/moderation and mobile-first accessibility

## Trust and monetisation
Separate seller claims from platform-verified facts. Encourage disclosure of faults, repairs, battery health where applicable, and accessories. Never fake verification, inspection, reviews, guarantees, or buyer protection. Possible future revenue streams include featured listings, dealer subscriptions, clearly labelled ads, or transaction services, subject to owner approval.

## Deferred unless approved
Payments, escrow/custody, physical inspection network, automated fraud scoring, AI phone recognition, push notifications, advanced analytics. Do not imply these are live. Billing requires pricing, operational, and legal review.
